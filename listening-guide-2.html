Listening Guide

Playlist:

  1. Drake. “Started From the Bottom” from Nothing Was The Same. OVO Sound: 2013
  2. 50 Cent. “Hustler's Ambition” from Get Rich Or Die Tryin'- The Original Motion Picture Soundtrack. G-Unit Records: 2005
  3. A$AP Rocky. “Peso” from LIVE.LOVE.A$AP. RCA Records: 2011
  4. A$AP Rocky. “Praise The Lord (feat. Skepta)” from TESTING. RCA Records: 2017
  5. A$AP Ferg. “Work REMIX (feat. A$AP Rocky, French Montana, Trinidad James & ScHoolboy Q)” from Trap Lord. RCA Records: 2013
  6. A$AP Ferg. “Plain Jane REMIX (feat. Nicki Minaj)” from Still Striving. RCA Records: 2017
  7. A$AP Ferg. “Jet Lag” from Floor Seats. RCA Records: 2019

Listening Guide:

The song opens with a light beat, instantly recognizable with any crowd. Then there is Ferg, spitting his famous line “Ride with the mob,Alhamdulillah”. The floor beneath you begins to creak under the weight of the crowd that surrounds you. Neon lights flash above as the beat drops, causing the crowd to move with it, chanting the lyrics in unison. “Plain Jane”, by A$AP Ferg, is undoubtedly an anthem. The high energy beat entrances the crowd to move as one, belting its vulgar but motivational lyrics. The song is recognizable by anyone in this generation, in any party scenario, but the song’s message extends far beyond that of a club banger. Paying homage to the song “Slob on my Knob”, by southern rap group Three6Mafia, “Plain Jane” carries themes of dedication, faith, and ambition, reflecting the songs and artists that inspired it, to tell a story of struggle, grind, and shine.

This playlist aims to bring the listener on the journey that Ferg describes in “Plain Jane”, while theming it in the context of a Harlem Picnic—life-or-death situations, which often involved drugs, from his childhood, which he references in “Plain Jane”. Together, the two themes will highlight the struggles, as well as the intensity of the environment many of these rappers came up in, as well as dissect some of A$AP Ferg’s motivations, messages, and signature: high intensity sound that gives him command of the dance scene. The playlist includes other works from A$AP Mob and pays homage to notable artists that shaped the rap genre. 

The playlist opens with Drake’s “Started From the Bottom”, which lays the groundwork for themes in perseverance and struggle. The song describes the hardships, such as poverty, violence, and abuse that Drake, and many others like him, experienced. The song also preaches the importance of togetherness and the grind mentality, alluding to many of the themes that will appear further in the playlist. The minimalist beat helps highlight the lyrics of the song: strive for success, and Drake draws parallels to Ferg in terms of their upbringing. The two both experienced struggles as children, from poverty to family issues. Likewise, however, the two experienced massive successes later on. As a result, both rap from experience. Lyrically, “Started From the Bottom” is similar to “Plain Jane” in that the two songs both repeat their titles, creating a hypnotic beat, and carrying the theme of perseverance, and paints a picture of the listener on the situation many of these rappers grow up in.

“Hustler’s Ambition” by fellow New York rapper 50 Cent takes the listener to the roots of rap. The song’s lyrics are gritty and determined, combined with a slow retro beat, works to convey its message of motivation and importance of dreams, which works in conjunction with the carelessness and “grind mentality” that “Plain Jane” perpetuates. 50 Cent paints a picture of people who don’t settle for anything less than the best, highlighting the hunger and relentlessness that many around him have. Rhythmically, the song is flowy, and carries a less bass-reliant beat, forming a nice, dreamy, transition between the childhood of the rappers and what is to come, taking a moment to touch upon what these rappers aspire to be, to break out of poverty, and indulge in life.

“Peso”, by fellow A$AP Mob member and Harlem rapper A$AP Rocky, consists of a hazy beat that mirrors the dizziness of being beaten up when being caught in a fight during a Harlem Picnic. There are moments in the song where a deep voice comes in that describes a graphic scene, which is very fitting for such a place in the playlist, almost as if between the confusion you get shots of adrenaline. The lyrics reflect the hard mentality many rappers have, and show the sacrifices that some made along the way to success, such as friends and education.

The playlist then moves to “Praise the Lord,” also by A$AP Rocky. It explores the motivations of many rappers and their connections to God. The song shows how many rappers attribute success to faith, as well as maintaining the message of perseverance through lyrics and beat. The bouncy rhythm of the song conveys a message of thanks and relief that one is meant to feel after being put in a life-or-death situation, such as the ones Ferg and his counterparts experienced in their past during Harlem Picnics. The listener, in the shoes of a rapper, can take a moment to reflect on what just happened, and the beat song serves as a transition to the high energy pieces that follow.

“Work” by A$AP Ferg, is one of the predecessors to his viral song “Plain Jane”. It preaches themes of sacrifice, violence, and trauma that he and many rappers had to go through on their journey up. There is a lot of mention of violence, death, and guns in the song, put on top of a heavy gangster beat that highlights the intensity of the situations these people had to face, as well as indicating a “loss of innocence” process that these rappers went through. It again brings the listener to another hardship that they must face, highlighting how the path to success is not straight. 

With “Plain Jane”, the main focus on the playlist, represents a final push to success for rappers, their breakthrough. The song brings the hardest beat, showing the A-game that these rappers need in order to create a song that can be enjoyed by the masses. Its lyrics sum up everything epitome of the grind, and the aggressive flow shows the amount of dominance rappers have over their audience and their genre when they finally succeed. Everything before sums up to this moment, and Ferg attributes his success to god.

“Jet Lag,” puts the focus back to Ferg. The listener, put in the shoes of notable rappers, made it. The swag, bassy beat paints the image of a king, supported by the song’s flashy lyrics about designer clothes, flashy jewelry, and fancy cars. The success that was alluded to earlier in the playlist was made a reality, through all of the hardships the rapper faced. The listener made it through those moments where they could have lost their life in the Harlem Picnic, signifying the hardships many of the rappers endured. The song maintains a high-intensity beat, showing the work hard, play hard mentality of these rappers. Although the listener has made it, they are reminded that they must continue working to stay at the top.

After being taken on this journey, “Plain Jane” is much more than just a party song meant to be blasted at groups of hyped young-adults, it embodies the history and culture of rap that glorifies perseverance, hard work, and relentless pursuit of success. This playlist is meant to embody such common values in rap, bring the listener through a story of success, as well as capture it in a way that reflects lived experiences from the rapper's childhoods, through the harlem picnic. Outside of the playlist, “Plain Jane” tells of its own story, alluding to the creator’s childhood and mentors. It has made a huge impact on social media as well as parties, highlighting the success that one can achieve when taken through such a process of struggle, grind, and shine.
